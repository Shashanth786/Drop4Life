Blood Donation Process

Follow these simple steps to make your donation safe and impactful.

Before Donation

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water before donating blood.

Get Enough Sleep

Sleep at least 7-8 hours before donation.

Eat Healthy

Have a balanced meal 2-3 hours before donating.

Avoid Alcohol & Smoking

Avoid alcohol for 24 hours and smoking before donation.

Carry Valid ID

Bring your government-issued identity proof.

What To Eat

Carbohydrates

Rice, Bread, Pasta, Oats and Cereals.

Fresh Fruits

Apples, Bananas, Oranges and Seasonal Fruits.

Protein Rich Foods

Eggs, Paneer, Dal, Chicken and Nuts.

Drink Fluids

Water, Fruit Juice or Coconut Water.

Eat nutritious food and stay hydrated for a safe donation.
After Donation

Stay Hydrated

Drink extra fluids for the next 24 hours.

Eat Well

Eat a nutritious meal after donating blood.

Take Rest

Relax for a few hours and avoid heavy exercise.

Avoid Heavy Lifting

Avoid lifting heavy objects for 24 hours.

Care For Bandage

Keep the bandage on and avoid touching the area.

Your small act of kindness can save a life. Thank You For Being A Hero!
